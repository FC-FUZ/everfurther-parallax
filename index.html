The Next Loop Endurance Series

One format. Endless ways to test yourself.

Every event in the series is built around a single, repeatable 5K trail loop with starts on the hour. There's no distance you have to commit to in advance — athletes decide how far they go, one loop at a time.

Two loops and you've run a 10K. Five earn you a 25K. Stay for all ten hourly loops and you walk away with a 50K ultra. Every completed loop counts, and every mile you earn is honored.

How it works

The loop format

  1. 01

    Everyone starts together

    Each loop begins on the hour, and the whole field toes the line — leaders and back-of-packers side by side.

  2. 02

    Run the loop

    One 5K circuit of trail. Supportive, visible, and fair — you're never far from aid or from the community.

  3. 03

    Decide again

    Make the cutoff and you've earned the choice: stop with the distance you've banked, or line up for one more loop.

  4. 04

    Repeat up to ten times

    A shared rhythm, hour after hour. Go as far as you choose within the event window — every loop is an achievement.

Who is the series for?

🌱

New runners

Building confidence or chasing a first 10K? The loop gives you structure, support, and a low-pressure way to find out what you can do.

Experienced runners

🔥

The ultra curious

Ready to find your edge? Keep lining up, hour after hour, and stop on your own terms. Every distance is earned — and honored.
